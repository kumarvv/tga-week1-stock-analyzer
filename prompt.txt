4. CSV SCHEMA AND SAMPLE FILE

Use:

transaction_id,date,account,source,symbol,transaction_type,quantity,price,fees,amount,currency

Create a downloadable sample containing:

transaction_id,date,account,source,symbol,transaction_type,quantity,price,fees,amount,currency
TX001,2025-01-02,Individual,CSV,,DEPOSIT,0,0,0,10000.00,USD
TX002,2025-01-03,Individual,CSV,AAPL,BUY,10,180.00,1.00,-1801.00,USD
TX003,2025-01-06,Individual,CSV,MSFT,BUY,5,400.00,1.00,-2001.00,USD
TX004,2025-03-14,Individual,CSV,AAPL,DIVIDEND,0,0,0,2.50,USD
TX005,2025-04-10,Individual,CSV,AAPL,SELL,2,200.00,1.00,399.00,USD
TX006,2025-04-15,Individual,CSV,,WITHDRAWAL,0,0,0,-500.00,USD

Document and enforce:
- Dates: YYYY-MM-DD.
- Stable transaction IDs, unique within source and account.
- Supported types: BUY, SELL, DIVIDEND, DEPOSIT, WITHDRAWAL, FEE.
- Positive quantities for BUY and SELL; fractional shares supported.
- Nonnegative prices and fees.
- amount represents signed net cash movement.
- BUY amount = -(quantity × price + fees).
- SELL amount = quantity × price - fees.
- DIVIDEND and DEPOSIT amounts are positive.
- WITHDRAWAL and FEE amounts are negative.
- Fees included in trades must not be deducted twice.
- Symbols required for BUY, SELL, and DIVIDEND.
- Symbols optional for cash-only transactions.
- USD only for the MVP; explain unsupported currencies.
- Validate amount consistency using a documented rounding tolerance.
- Preserve input order when transactions share a date.

Support optional opening positions:

account,symbol,quantity,total_cost_basis,as_of_date,currency

Provide an opening cash input per account.

Explain that opening positions and cash are required when history does not start from an empty account. Do not infer missing cost basis.

7. FINANCIAL CALCULATIONS

Implement and document:
- Holdings from transactions and opening positions.
- Cash from opening cash and signed cash movements.
- FIFO realized gains, calculated separately by account and symbol.
- Purchase fees included in acquisition cost.
- Sale fees deducted from proceeds.
- Remaining cost basis after partial sales.
- Unrealized gain/loss = market value - remaining cost basis.
- Unrealized gain/loss percentage = gain/loss / remaining cost basis.
- N/A for zero denominators.
- Net external contributions = deposits minus withdrawals.
- Dividends tracked separately from trading gains.

Use the complete available history to establish positions and cost basis before filtering reporting periods.

The MVP supports long-only stocks and ETFs.
- Prevent or flag sales exceeding available shares.
- Detect and flag unsupported options, shorts, transfers, splits, and other corporate actions.
- Do not silently treat unsupported activity as ordinary trades.

For incomplete history:
- Prefer reliable broker-provided positions and cost basis where available.
- Show differences between reconstructed and broker-reported holdings.
- Mark unreliable calculations as incomplete or unavailable.
- Do not fabricate missing balances, basis, or performance.
